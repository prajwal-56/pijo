# ⚡ PIJO — The Autonomous Team Orchestrator
> **Stop guessing who does what. Allocate by skills.**

Welcome to **PIJO**, your AI-powered task management command center! This guide will walk you through how to use the platform to effortlessly manage your hackathon projects, sprint backlogs, and team assignments using the power of Google Gemini AI.

---

## 🎯 1. The Dashboard (Mission Control)
When you open PIJO, you'll land on the **Dashboard**. This is your high-level executive overview.
- **Sprint Velocity Meter**: Watch this striped progress bar fill up as your team crushes tasks.
- **AI Project Health Summary**: Get a quick, AI-generated readout of how the sprint is going.
- **Live Telemetry**: See exactly how many tasks are Done, In Progress, or Unassigned at a glance.
- **Quick Actions**: Use the vibrant quick-launch buttons to run AI assignments or jump straight to the task board.

---

## 👥 2. Onboarding Your Team (Members Page)
PIJO's superpower is matching tasks to people based on their actual skills. To do this, PIJO needs to know your team.

1. Navigate to the **Members** page using the sidebar.
2. Under **New Teammate**, enter the person's name and a short bio.
3. **Upload a Resume (The Magic Step!)**: Upload a `.md`, `.txt`, or `.pdf` version of their resume or skill sheet.
4. Click **Create Member**.
5. **Watch the AI work**: Click the **"🤖 Extract Skills"** button on their member card. PIJO will read their resume and generate verifiable skill tags (e.g., `React`, `Python`, `Figma`). 

> 💡 *Tip: The better the resume you provide, the smarter PIJO's task assignments will be!*

---

## 📋 3. Loading the Backlog (Task Board)
Now that your team is ready, let's give them some work.

1. Navigate to the **Task Board**.
2. **Add tasks manually**: Click **"➕ Add Task"** to type in a title, description, and initial priority.
3. **Bulk Upload (Fastest!)**: Click **"📁 Upload CSV"** to instantly ingest a massive backlog of tasks. (Your CSV should have `title` and `description` columns).

---

## 🤖 4. Unleashing the AI Engine
This is where PIJO handles the heavy lifting so you don't have to.

### ⚡ Auto-Assignment
Click the hot pink **"🤖 AI Assign All"** button on the Task Board. 
PIJO will analyze every unassigned task, cross-reference it against the extracted skills of every team member, and assign it to the perfect person. It even generates a **💡 AI Fit** speech bubble on the task card explaining *why* they were chosen!

### 📊 Auto-Prioritization
Have a massive backlog and don't know what to tackle first? Click the purple **"📊 Prioritize"** button. PIJO will read the context of every task and re-label them as `🌱 Low`, `🔹 Medium`, `⚡ High`, or `🔥 Critical`.

---

## 🏃 5. Crushing the Sprint (Kanban Flow)
As your team works, keep the board updated!
- Change a task's lane using the dropdown on the card (To Do 🟡 ➔ In Progress 🔵 ➔ Done 🟢).
- **Pro-tip**: Try moving a task to **Done** and enjoy the celebratory confetti explosion! 🎉
- You can manually override any AI assignment by using the Assignee dropdown on any task card.

---

## 💬 6. Your Personal AI Project Manager
Stuck on a decision? Need to know who has bandwidth? 
Click the floating **"Ask PIJO AI"** button in the bottom right corner of any page.

You can ask things like:
- *"Who is the best person on the team to handle database migrations?"*
- *"Are there any critical tasks currently blocked?"*
- *"Give me a summary of what Sarah is working on."*

PIJO understands your entire project state in real-time and will reply with beautifully formatted, contextual advice.
